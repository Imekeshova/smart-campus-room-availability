All findings are owned by **Imekeshova Malika**, the project maintainer and release approver. L is estimated likelihood from 1 (unlikely) to 3 (likely in normal maintenance or use). I is estimated impact from 1 (limited rework), through 2 (incorrect internal handling), to 3 (misleading user verdict). Score = L × I. Scores are engineering judgements, not empirical incident rates. Priority follows the two highest confirmed starting scores. Files below are relative to the source tree unless an evidence path is stated.
